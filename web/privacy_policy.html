Privacy Policy for Play & Earn

Last Updated: May 30, 2026

Play & Earn ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This India-only Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application ("Play & Earn").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access or use the application.

1. Information We Collect

To provide secure social rewards, fraud prevention, and redemption review, we collect the following categories of data:

Personal Identifying Information (PII)

  • Account Credentials: Display name, email address, profile picture, and authentication tokens (when registering an email account or signing in via Google Sign-In).
  • Payment Address & Payout Details: UPI ID, gift card email address, and complete transaction histories necessary to review eligible promotional redemption requests.
  • Verification Metadata: Confirmed email addresses, phone numbers, and authentication status used strictly for verification during redemption requests to prevent multi-accounting fraud.

Device and Gameplay Data

  • Technical Device Identifiers: Device ID, Operating System (OS) type and version, language settings, and advertising identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID / GAID, Apple ID for Advertisers / IDFA) for attribution and ad monetization.
  • App Activity & Analytics: Gameplay metrics (wins, losses, match history), current coin balance, daily login streaks, lucky spins, and self-exclusion preferences.
  • Network Quality Details: Latency (ping) and temporary IP address records to maintain ELO-based matchmaking quality.

2. Third-Party Data Processing

To support our virtual economy, ads, and survey rewards, we integrate with third-party service providers. These companies may collect, process, and store your data in accordance with their respective privacy policies:

  • Supabase (Database & Auth): Serves as our primary database, authentication portal, and secure backend provider. All user data is secured by Postgres Row Level Security (RLS). Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Google Sign-In: Authenticates users and retrieves verified email addresses. Google Privacy Policy
  • Monetization & Ads (ironSource / AdMob / Unity Ads): Delivers rewarded, banner, and interstitial ads. These networks collect device advertising IDs to deliver personalized, relevant ads. ironSource Privacy Policy
  • Surveys & Offerwalls (CPX Research / BitLabs): Enables survey monetization. To qualify you for relevant surveys, an encrypted hash of your user ID, along with approximate geographical location and device settings, is sent to these partners.
    • CPX Research Privacy Policy
    • BitLabs Privacy Policy

3. How We Use Your Information

We process and utilize your information under strict legal bases to:

  • Establish and secure your player account.
  • Manage and track your coin balances, rewarded transactions, and in-game ELO.
  • Review eligible promotional redemption requests safely.
  • Enforce anti-fraud protections, preventing VPN/proxy abuse, cheating, bots, and duplicate accounts.
  • Host, maintain, and optimize app performance and bug report tickets.

4. Data Sharing & Security

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We only share information with service providers bound by confidentiality, or when legally required to prevent fraud, protect rights, or comply with statutory law. All transmission of sensitive personal data (e.g., email, UPI ID, transaction data) is encrypted in transit using SSL/TLS protocols.

5. GDPR & CCPA Data Rights (Data Deletion)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the right to access, correct, or permanently delete your personal information.

Complete Account Deletion

You can request the immediate and permanent deletion of your account and all associated identifiers (email, name, UPI ID, ELO records, coin history) directly in the app:

  1. Navigate to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap App Settings.
  3. Select Delete Account under the Danger Zone.
  4. Confirm by entering your exact profile name.

All your personal identifying data is immediately and permanently expunged from our database servers.

6. Children's Privacy

Play & Earn is restricted to users who are at least 18 years of age and located in India. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children or minors. If you believe a minor has created an account, please contact us immediately.
