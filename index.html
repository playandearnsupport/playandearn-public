About Play and Earn

Play and Earn is an Android application that allows users to access interactive features, games, rewards, and account-based services. The application is developed using Flutter and is designed to provide a secure and user-friendly experience.

Google Sign-In

Play and Earn uses Google Sign-In to authenticate users securely. When users choose to sign in with their Google account, the application may access basic profile information such as name, email address, and profile picture for account creation, authentication, and account management.

Play and Earn does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, Google Photos, or other sensitive Google services unless explicitly stated and approved by the user.
